The rules

1. Open claim: any account claims 1,000 IMEME once, first come, 1,000 claims max. No team tokens and no owner mint. One claim per account, so it is not Sybil-resistant.
2. Burn-to-prompt: pay IMEME to ask the brain. Your tokens wait in on-chain escrow (NEAR yield/resume). Answered: half burned, half to the pot. No answer in time: full refund. Each prompt raises the price 10%; it cools hourly.
3. Ritual: the brain posts a challenge, holders pay an entry fee into the pot, the best entry scoring 60+ takes it all. No winner: the pot rolls over.
